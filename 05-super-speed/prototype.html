Proposal for 4.3 · starvation group only

One bad week shouldn't silence a responder for good.

Meteor Mite starts this demo where the data left them on 31 Aug: pinned at the 0.0 floor, with no offers coming in to earn it back. Use the controls at the bottom and watch the score, the log and Kip's console move together.

+
Accept · +0.08
Unchanged from today. Counts as sent and taken.
−
Decline or timeout · −0.12
Unchanged, scored the same. Both count as sent but not taken, and each is logged separately, so a missed offer is never confused with a "no".
↑
Weekly floor lift NEW
Once a week, any score below 0.5 moves halfway toward 0.5, offers or not. It pulls toward neutral, not the ceiling, so a habitual decliner doesn't get a clean record.
◉
Ping-received log NEW
Every offer that reaches the device shows up as a notification and a log entry, even when it times out. Silence always has an explanation.
★
Handler priority NEW
If a responder was sent at least 50% fewer pings than the average responder last week, their handler can put them first in line for the next callout. It's used once, doesn't change the score, and is recorded in the routing-override audit log.
Where this lives: a new Reliability tab in the Responder mobile app, next to Offers and Availability. The same data appears in the Handler console under the responder detail panel.
